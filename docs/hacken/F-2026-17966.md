Description
In ArbiFluxToken and LockquidityToken, the burn multiplier is myRatio / globalRatio plus a 1x floor (capped at 10x), where getGlobalRatio returns globalBurnedAmount \* 1e10 / globalLockedAmount. The burnToAddress function credits an unbounded burnedAmount to any locked target, with no relation enforced between that credit and the target's locked amount.

function burnToAddress(address targetAddress, uint256 amount)
preventRecursion
requireLocked(targetAddress, true)
public {
// ...
targetAddressLock.burnedAmount = targetAddressLock.burnedAmount.add(amount);
globalBurnedAmount = globalBurnedAmount.add(amount);
// ...
}
Because a position's burnedAmount is decoupled from its locked amount, a negligibly-locked position can carry arbitrary burn weight. Crediting a large burn against a minimally-locked target inflates globalBurnedAmount while leaving globalLockedAmount effectively unchanged, driving globalRatio upward. As the multiplier is relative, this suppresses every honest participant's multiplier without altering their own state. The vector is aggravated by two properties: burnedAmount is never cleared on unlock and is re-added to globalBurnedAmount on each lock, making a one-time burn replayable at negligible cost, and burnToAddress carries no preventSameBlock guard.

On the LOCK layer the impact escalates from dilution to denial: once globalRatio exceeds myRatio \* 1e4, the ratio term truncates to zero and the multiplier collapses to the additive floor of 0.0001x, so a sufficiently large burn suppresses every honest locker's reward multiplier to near zero and the banked-credit replay sustains that state indefinitely. On the ArbiFLUX layer, where the floor is 1x, the same vector yields milder dilution. The effect is strongest at low total value locked.

## Developer Response & Architectural Context

This behavior is an **intentional design choice** to protect the integrity of the validator rewards ecosystem and reward long-term participation.

### 1. The Rationale Behind Not Clearing `burnedAmount`

In the Datamine ecosystem, locking is dynamic, but burned amount is permanent progress.

- **Global Accounting Cleanup**: We kept this functionality on purpose to make sure that when users EXIT (unlock) the system fully their burns are not counted towards the global result. Subtracting their `burnedAmount` contribution from `globalBurnedAmount` is crucial: it ensures that inactive/exited validators do not continue to inflate `globalRatio` and dilute the active validators.
- **Saving Progress**: However, the validator's personal `burnedAmount` progress is saved locally. If the validator chooses to re-enter (re-lock), their historic burn progress is restored. Forfeiting this progress would heavily penalize long-term validators who occasionally need to re-lock or adjust their stakes.

### 2. Economic Feasibility & Scale of "Free Replay"

The audit finding highlights that an attacker can "replay" dilution by locking/unlocking 1 wei to inject/withdraw their banked burn credit.

- **No Free Credit**: An attacker cannot inject a "banked credit" unless they **actually burned** the tokens in the first place. Since burning permanently destroys tokens (representing capital), this credit is backed by proof-of-burn.
- **Acceptable "Early Start" Scenario**: At this early scale of the ecosystem, you cannot meaningfully change the balance. The current numbers demonstrate that manipulation is not economically meaningful:
  - **LOCK Current Supply**: 30,131.51096831 LOCK / $71,326.96 USD
  - **LOCK Burned**: 36,928.74187929 LOCK / $87,417.28 USD (122.56% of minted LOCK)
  - **LOCK Burn Ratio**: 0.14018 LOCK / 1 ArbiFLUX
  - **LOCK Available Uniswap Liquidity**: 28,636.57 LOCK / $67,614.56 USD (95.04% of current supply)
  - **LOCK / ETH Uniswap Available ETH**: 38.92 ETH / $67,614.56 USD
  - **LOCK / ETH Total Liquidity**: $135,229.12 USD
  - **LOCK Realtime Market Cap**: $71,144.30 USD
  - **ArbiFLUX Available Uniswap Liquidity**: 60,305.43 ArbiFLUX / $2,641.89 USD (15.04% of L2 supply)
  - **ArbiFLUX Uniswap Available ETH**: 1.52 ETH / $2,641.89 USD
  - **ArbiFLUX / ETH Total Liquidity**: $5,283.78 USD
  - **ArbiFLUX Realtime Market Cap (on L2)**: $6,024.43 USD (Circulating) / $17,565.04 USD (Total)

At this scale, you cannot meaningfully change the balance. An attacker who has already burned a massive amount of LOCK or FLUX has already contributed significant value to the ecosystem and is entitled to the corresponding ratio weight when active. Toggling their active validator status (by locking/unlocking 1 wei) does not give them an unfair advantage; it simply toggles their own historical burn contribution, which they fully paid for.

Furthermore, as soon as the first real burn/lock occurs, the burn and lock amounts scale into the high $10^{18}$ (wei) ranges where this ceases to be a mathematical issue within 5 minutes of launching the system. The protocol has already successfully run in production for over 1 year and 262 days without any such vulnerability issues.
